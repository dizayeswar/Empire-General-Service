# Phone tutorial — line manager and director

Two short films, about 60–90 seconds each. Film in portrait on a phone. Use a test account and a practice paper.

Show **Select** and the **Confirm selected** button. Do not confirm a real employee’s leave on camera. Stop before you press Confirm, or use a practice paper only.

The same words are in the phone app under **Help**. Switch the phone language in Settings if you are filming Arabic or Sorani. The leave paper itself stays in English.

## Line manager

1. Open Leave request and sign in as the line manager.
2. Point to the bottom bar. Say: “Leave requests is where HR puts papers assigned to you.”
3. Show a practice paper: name, dates, and days.
4. Tap **Select**, then tick that paper. Say: “Tick only the papers you will sign.”
5. Point to **Confirm selected**. Do not press it. Say: “Confirm selected puts your e-signature in the Line Manager box only, then sends the paper to the director.”
6. If this account is also an employee, say: “If the phone asks which box, choose Line Manager only. The stamp goes in that one box.”
7. Point to **Archive**. Say: “After you confirm, the paper stays here. Pending means the director has not signed yet.”
8. Point to **My employees**. Say: “These are the people assigned to you.”

## Director

1. Open Leave request and sign in as the director.
2. Point to **Leave requests** on the bottom bar. Say: “A paper appears here after the line manager signs. If there is no line manager, it comes straight from HR.”
3. Tap **Select**, then tick the practice paper.
4. Point to **Confirm selected**. Do not press it. Say: “Confirm selected puts your e-signature in the Director box only.”
5. Point to **Director confirmed**. Say: “After you confirm, the paper goes back to HR as a completed request and is listed here. Once it has left your inbox, the stamp cannot be moved.”
